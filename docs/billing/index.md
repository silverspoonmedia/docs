# Billing & Subscriptions

Silverspoon is funded by paid plans and add-ons. This section explains what you can buy, how a purchase works, and how to pay.

Everything you buy is attached to your account rather than to a single product, so a storage plan bought for Archivd is visible from the same catalog that sells a Microstock workspace slot.

## The short version

1. Open the **Services** catalog and pick what you need.
2. Choose the currency you want to be charged in and any quantity, then start the checkout.
3. On the purchase page, choose a payment method and pay.
4. Your entitlement activates, and the product unlocks.

## Plans and add-ons

Two shapes of purchase exist:

| Kind | Behavior |
|------|----------|
| Main plan | One at a time. Buying a new main plan replaces the current one, with unused time credited toward the new plan. |
| Add-on | Stackable. Each unit adds to what you already have. |

Add-ons renew on their own anniversary, which is why the catalog may ask you to settle a pending add-on renewal before buying more units.

## When a plan ends

Paid entitlements do not simply stop. They move through a short lifecycle:

| State | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Active | Paid and usable. |
| Grace | The period has ended, and you have a short window to renew. |
| Expired | The grace window closed and the account fell back to the free tier. |

During grace, uploads and collaborative features are blocked but your data is kept. If grace closes while you are over the free allowance, the excess is trimmed. The practical advice is simple: renew during grace, or remove files you no longer need.

:::tip
Plan endings are announced in your [notifications](/docs/notifications), so you get a heads-up before grace begins rather than after.
:::

## Payments and tax

Payment routing and tax obligations differ depending on where you are. That is covered in [Payments and Taxation](/docs/pembayaran-dan-perpajakan), which explains the domestic and international paths.
