# Authentication

Authentication covers the actions related to account access rights and the features tied to them.

:::info[TL;DR]
This section explains the authentication features of vTual.
:::

## Register

To start using vTual, both viewers and content creators must [register](https://www.vtual.net/auth/register) first.

![vTual registration page](/assets/docs/vtual/Auth_Register.webp)

After completing registration, you receive an email with instructions to verify your email address, which grants additional features on the platform. You can also skip email verification if you choose not to complete that step.

By registering, you agree to the [Terms](/docs/legal/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/docs/legal/privacy).

## Login

To fully utilize most of the features offered by vTual, start by accessing your account through the [login](https://www.vtual.net/auth/login) page.

![vTual login page](/assets/docs/vtual/Auth_Login.webp)

Logging in unlocks all available functionality and improves your overall experience compared to browsing as a guest.

:::note
Currently, you can only log in using an account you have registered before. Logging in with external accounts such as Gmail, Twitter, or Facebook is not yet available in version 0.1.0.
:::

## Email verification

The email verification link is only valid for **30 minutes** after you complete registration. After that, the link expires and can no longer be used.

![vTual email verification](/assets/docs/vtual/Auth_Email_Verify.webp)

If you decide to verify later, request a new verification link through the **Resend Verification Code** menu. That option lets you obtain a fresh link and complete the process at your convenience.

One benefit of verifying your email is account recovery, in case you lose access due to a forgotten password.

## Account recovery

There may be times when you lose access to your account because you forgot your password.

![vTual account recovery](/assets/docs/vtual/Auth_Email_Recover_Password_Lost.webp)

To help with this, we provide an [account recovery](https://www.vtual.net/auth/recover) feature so you can regain access to your account and continue using our services without hassle.

## Reset password

If your account recovery request is approved, you can then reset your password, which becomes your new credential.

![vTual password reset](/assets/docs/vtual/Auth_Email_Recover_Password_Reset.webp)

This process lets you securely regain access to your account and continue using the platform.

:::note
Registration, sign-in, two-factor authentication, connected accounts, and password recovery are platform-wide, not specific to vTual. The pages above show the screens you see when you arrive from vTual; for the full explanation, see [Account & Security](/docs/account/).
:::
