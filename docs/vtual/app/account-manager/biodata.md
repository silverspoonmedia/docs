# Biodata

To give more information and context about a user, vTual provides the [Biodata menu](https://www.vtual.net/apps/account-manager/biodata). This feature lets users share additional details about themselves, enhancing their profiles and helping the community connect on a deeper level.

![vTual biodata settings](/assets/docs/vtual/App_Biodata.webp)

:::warning
The name field must be filled out accurately to meet the lore requirements, while other sections can be customized according to personal preferences.
:::

While filling out your biodata, make sure to follow the applicable guidelines.
