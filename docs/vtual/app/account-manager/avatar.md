# Avatar

As a project with a semi-social media niche, vTual lets you customize your avatar to make your profile more unique and appealing. You can do this through the [Avatar menu](https://www.vtual.net/apps/account-manager/avatar).

![vTual avatar settings](/assets/docs/vtual/App_Avatar.webp)

The avatar is then used throughout the entire platform, appearing in all sections and features that require an avatar display. In certain areas, such as channels, the avatar associated with that specific channel is used instead.

## Requirements

Everyone is eligible to use the avatar feature, as long as the file meets these requirements:

- Any known image format (jpg, png, webp, and so on).
- At least **5 KB** and no larger than **512 KB**.

:::note
To maintain avatar quality, uploaded images are not compressed or optimized in any way.
:::

If you choose not to customize your avatar, your account automatically uses a default avatar.
