# Content

Just like the [content](/docs/vtual/content) available on the main page, the content in the Simp menu is divided into [Live](https://www.vtual.net/apps/simp/live), [Scheduled](https://www.vtual.net/apps/simp/scheduled), [Archived](https://www.vtual.net/apps/simp/archived), and [Uploaded](https://www.vtual.net/apps/simp/uploaded) content.

![vTual simp live view](/assets/docs/vtual/Simp_Live.webp)

The significant difference is that the content displayed in these menus consists only of content from the Oshis you follow.

That means you can directly enjoy all the content from your Oshis across various services, making it easier to stay updated with their latest activity.
