# About vTual

Welcome to vTual, a project presented by Silverspoon. Before I start the journey, here is a brief introduction to what vTual is and what it offers you—without requiring a ceremonial onboarding montage.

## Background

It began during COVID-19. At the time, I was a fresh graduate with a keen interest in vTubers.

Just like today, most vTubers operated across several different platforms, which made it challenging for a first-time vTuber enthusiast who tended to focus on just one platform. If a vTuber was not active on one platform, it was easy to assume they were offline, only to find out later that they were streaming somewhere else.

The goal was simple: simplify tracking these streaming activities. So, as a naive fresh graduate programmer, the attempt was to build a portal that could monitor vTuber activity across various platforms. Ambitious, admittedly. Also a perfectly reasonable way to discover how many problems fit inside one idea.

The project was initially named **VNS**, short for "Virtual Network Services."

## A long pause

The effort proved challenging and took an exceptionally long time, largely due to limited coding experience as a fresh graduate.

In the middle of development, a job landed that demanded full attention, from 8 to 4. As a result, the project was gradually halted and eventually abandoned, a victim of limited experience and time.

So there was no further development for VNS over the next few years.

That is, until about four years later. In 2024, layoffs in the IT sector became something of a trend, and unfortunately I was affected too.

To maintain sanity, an escape was needed. Despite everything, the choice was to continue the late VNS project while using the experience gained from years of working as a developer. VNS development was rebooted in early 2024.

Redeveloping the project was no easy task. The existing codebase was poorly organized and low quality, which made the process feel like starting from scratch. A thrilling tour of past decisions, none of which came with receipts. Fortunately, some ideas from the main core were still usable, which significantly sped up the rapid development that followed.

Miraculously, the project was completed successfully. After some consideration, VNS was renamed the **vTual Project**.

## Terminology

As with most projects in the virtual content creator niche, the "v" in "vTual" stands for "virtual," while "Tual" itself does not hold any special meaning. Look closer, though, and "vTual" can also be read as "v(ir)Tual," or simply "virtual," which aligns with its niche and initial idea: vTuber activity tracking.

Even though the focus is on virtual content creators, non-vTuber content creators are also invited to use the service. Some features may feel a little *chuunibyou*-ish and otaku-like, but you can simply ignore those.

## Goals

vTual aims to bridge the gap between viewers and content creators. It makes it easier for viewers to find appealing content and for creators to expand their reach. By improving discoverability and accessibility, vTual enriches the viewer experience and supports creators in growing their audience.

- **For content creators:** join the vTual community so your content can be featured and seen by a larger audience. Becoming part of the community helps you expand your reach and engage with more viewers.
- **For viewers:** join the vTual community to receive the latest updates about your favorite creators, or discover new and promising ones. Stay informed and explore content that matches your interests.

## Verdict

This project also serves as an opportunity to build an impressive portfolio, because skills can be proven better with real applications than with a line on a CV.

By developing vTual, I am not only honing technical abilities but also giving back to a community that has provided so much enjoyment and inspiration. Through this endeavor, the hope is to showcase those capabilities and make a meaningful impact within the vTuber and content creator community in general.

So this project allows me to contribute something valuable to the community using my skills. Anything beyond that is a bonus.

With love, really.
