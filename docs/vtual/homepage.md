# Home Page

The home page is the main entry point when you first open vTual. It gathers the most useful information about each section in one place.

![vTual home page](/assets/docs/vtual/Homepage.webp)

## What it shows

- A list of content creators who are currently live streaming.
- Upcoming live streaming events.

Each entry displays important information such as the title, duration, number of concurrent viewers, and the streaming service used by the creator, such as Twitch or YouTube.

## Customizing the view

The content displayed can be tailored through **Content Preferences**. You can adjust settings such as the time zone display and the sorting options for live or archived content, scheduled events, and Video on Demand (VOD).

## Looking ahead

As the face of the site, this page is likely to change significantly in the future. Upcoming updates aim to improve the experience and keep the content library fresh and engaging.
