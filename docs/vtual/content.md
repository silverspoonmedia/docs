# Content

The content feature groups everything into a few content types based on their nature: **Live**, **Scheduled**, **Archived**, and **Uploaded**.

![vTual content page](/assets/docs/vtual/Content.webp)

Each entry displays similar important information, such as the title, duration, number of concurrent viewers, and the streaming service used by the creator, such as Twitch or YouTube. Every category serves a specific purpose, so you can quickly jump to the type of content you are looking for.

## Requirements

Only **verified channels** have their content displayed on the vTual system. For details about the channel verification process, see [External Link](/docs/vtual/app/account-manager/link#verify-a-link).

## Content types

| Type | What it is | Available from |
|------|------------|----------------|
| Live | Streams that are being broadcast right now. | Twitch, YouTube |
| Scheduled | Streams scheduled for a future broadcast. | YouTube |
| Archived | Streams that have ended and are now public. | Twitch, YouTube |
| Uploaded (VOD) | Videos uploaded directly, such as videos or shorts, without a premiere or live stream. | YouTube |

### Live

Active live streaming content that is currently being broadcast can be accessed through the **Live** menu. Viewers can easily find and join ongoing streams so they do not miss any exciting moments.

### Scheduled

Content scheduled for future live streaming broadcasts can be accessed through the **Scheduled** menu. Viewers can see upcoming streams and plan ahead.

### Archived

Once a live broadcast has concluded and the content is made available to the public, it can be accessed through the **Archived** menu. Viewers can revisit completed streams and catch up on anything they missed during the live broadcast.

### Uploaded (VOD)

Content uploaded directly, without going through a premiere or being part of a live stream, such as uploaded videos or shorts, is categorized as **Uploaded VOD** (Video on Demand). This content can be accessed through the **Uploaded** menu, so viewers can find videos that are readily available at any time.

## Preference

You can customize your content viewing preferences through the **Content Preferences** menu.

![vTual content preferences](/assets/docs/vtual/Preference_Content.webp)

This lets you tailor the types of content you see for a more personalized experience.

:::note
These preferences are stored in your browser's cookies. Make sure your browser is set to accept cookies from vTual for this feature to work.
:::

## Data handling

vTual only stores and displays content that is accessible to the public. If a piece of content becomes unavailable, such as when it is set to private, restricted to members only, or deleted, then vTual removes all related data from our system.

:::info[Scope of "content"]
Here, "content" refers to the data and information that vTual obtains from related services, such as Twitch or YouTube, which is then processed and stored in our internal database.
:::

When vTual states that it removes content that is not available to the public, it does not mean vTual can delete your content from any service. We do not have the ability or authority to do that.

> **TL;DR:** We cannot delete your Twitch or YouTube content from your Twitch or YouTube account. Removal refers only to our internal database, so we retain and show only content that is accessible to the public.
