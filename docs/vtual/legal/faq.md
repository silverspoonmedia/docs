# Frequently Asked Questions

This section answers the questions that are likely to be asked most often about vTual. Consider this a head start before you have to ask.

:::info[TL;DR]
A collection of the vTual-specific questions we expect to come up frequently, answered up front.
:::

## What is this project?

vTual simplifies the process for content creators to promote their content, ensuring it reaches both their loyal audience and potential new viewers.

Viewers are also free to customize the content they want to see. One of the key features is the ability to follow favorite content creators, as well as those they might enjoy in the future.

The project is not dedicated to any specific fandom; it welcomes all content creators to use the platform. In the initial stages, however, the focus is likely to be on vTubers as the primary content creators within the system.

## Which services are tracked?

Currently, vTual only tracks and monitors the activity of verified channels from:

- **Twitch** (profile, streaming)
- **YouTube** (profile, streaming, past VOD, video, short)

Read the vTual [External Link documentation](/docs/vtual/app/account-manager/link) for updates about tracker verification.

Additional services may be introduced based on user requests and needs. We are committed to listening to our community and adapting our offerings to serve you better. By actively seeking feedback and understanding evolving preferences, we aim to enhance the platform and provide features that truly resonate with your requirements.

## When was this project created?

The idea, concept, and initial development of this project began on **October 10, 2022**.

However, due to various challenges, primarily the complexity of the project exceeding capabilities at the time, along with other work commitments, the project was ultimately put on hold indefinitely.

Fast forward to March 2024, development was rebooted. This time, it leveraged the same ideas and concepts but with a completely new codebase written from scratch.

The public launch is scheduled to take place around Q1 of 2025. This timeline allows us to ensure adequate testing, refine the product, and address potential issues before making it available to a wider audience.

## Why was this project created?

Simply because:

- We love content creators, especially vTubers.
- We want to grow the pool of vTubers we can watch.
- Tracking and switching between streaming services to watch favorite vTubers is a hassle.
- If possible, it serves as a means to build more connections, whether from the vTuber community, the general content creator community, or even the programmer community.
- It is a means to develop programming skills so as not to get trapped in tutorial hell and become rusty over time.
- It helps overcome impostor syndrome, especially after being labeled as someone who cannot perform well.
- Most importantly, we need a somewhat complex web application as a portfolio to land a more interesting job compared to the previous one.

The motivations behind a programmer embarking on a project often stem from personal challenges and needs, and this project is no exception.

## Is this project a clone?

There are currently several similar projects that offer key features focused on tracking the activities of favorite content creators. However, we would not go so far as to say these projects are mere clones of one another; vTual is not their clone, nor are they vTual's clone.

Unlike the file hosting market, where the codebase is publicly available through paid web apps such as [XFilesharing](https://sibsoft.net/xfilesharing.html) and [Yetishare](https://yetishare.com), the market for projects similar to vTual remains largely proprietary.

This means that anyone wishing to develop a project of this nature currently needs to build it from the ground up, which is a big and difficult step.

Some developers may specialize in creating these projects using content management systems (CMS) like WordPress and its various plugins, while others may feel more comfortable using frameworks such as Laravel or CodeIgniter. Each approach has its own advantages and challenges, allowing developers to leverage their strengths and preferences.

Given the nature of this market, we believe it is somewhat unethical to claim that one project is simply copying another, as each initiative operates within its own unique context and is often driven by different goals, user needs, and creative visions.

Moreover, as the number of similar projects increases, the real beneficiaries are content creators and their audiences. Each project developer is motivated to actively enhance their platform by adding features that attract both creators and viewers, fostering a competitive environment.

This healthy competition encourages innovation and improvement, ensuring users have access to a variety of options tailored to their needs. As a result, the risk of a single platform monopolizing the market diminishes, allowing for a more dynamic and diverse ecosystem.

## What is your stance on problematic content creators?

In a large community, it is undeniable that problematic individuals will emerge, bringing challenges to the community as a whole. Unfortunately, this reality is vividly illustrated within the vTuber community, which has been marked by various issues and dramas.

As a platform for content creators and their fans, we are committed to addressing these issues in a professional manner without being swayed by emotions or the prevailing atmosphere. Therefore, vTual will only respond once a consensus has been reached regarding any problem.

Because we only want to recommend content creators who can provide a positive impact, if a content creator is found to have committed an offense widely opposed by the community, they will be removed from the platform.

## What about the interface quality?

There are no issues with the UI kits used for the UI design; the real challenge lies in our own limitations when it comes to UI design. We have a stronger grasp of backend development compared to frontend, which means the release of version 0.1 will utilize a rather basic UI.

If you are interested, we would greatly appreciate your contributions toward a more suitable UI. You are welcome to design your own interface or even purchase templates, UI kits, and other UI tools that you think would enhance this project.

Reach out to us about these inquiries, as your input could make a significant difference in improving the overall user experience. We look forward to collaborating with you on this aspect.
