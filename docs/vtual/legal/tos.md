# vTual Product Terms

These product terms cover what is specific to vTual. They supplement the platform [Terms of Service](/docs/legal/terms), which governs your use of vTual and every other Silverspoon product.

:::info[Summary]
The platform Terms of Service apply in full. This page only adds the rules that exist because vTual tracks content on third-party platforms.
:::

## Platform Terms apply

By using vTual, you agree to the platform [Terms of Service](/docs/legal/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/docs/legal/privacy). Those documents apply in full to vTual.

If anything on this page conflicts with the platform Terms of Service, the platform Terms of Service takes precedence, except where a rule here is genuinely specific to vTual and the platform document has no equivalent.

## Data and content rules

vTual is a catalogue of content creators and their published content. The following apply specifically to it:

- **Public data only.** vTual only displays channel and content information that the platform itself makes publicly available. It does not expose private, restricted, or paywalled material.
- **Official APIs only.** Data is retrieved exclusively through the official Twitch and YouTube APIs, never by scraping or by other unofficial means.
- **Content ownership stays with the creator.** The content you create remains yours. vTual does not claim copyright over your videos, streams, or any other material you publish on a third-party platform.

For how data is read and how to withdraw access, see the [Privacy Notice: Third-Party Platforms](/docs/vtual/legal/privacy).
