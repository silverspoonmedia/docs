# Privacy Notice: Third-Party Platforms

This notice covers the parts of vTual that rely on third-party platforms. It supplements the platform [Privacy Policy](/docs/legal/privacy), which explains how Silverspoon handles information generally.

:::info[TL;DR]
vTual reads publicly available channel and content data through the official Twitch and YouTube APIs. It never asks for your platform credentials, and you can revoke access or request deletion at any time.
:::

## YouTube API Services

vTual uses **YouTube API Services** to obtain publicly available information about tracked channels and their content.

By using vTual, you are agreeing to be bound by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). Google's handling of your information is described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## What we read from each platform

We obtain publicly available data about channels and content through the services below:

- [Twitch API Service](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/api)
  - Channel information about Channel ID, Name, Avatar, Banner, Description and Join Date.
  - Video information about ID, Title, Description, Publish Date, Duration.
- [YouTube API Service](https://developers.google.com/youtube)
  - Channel information about Channel ID, Playlist ID, Name, Avatar, Banner, Description, Number of View, Join Date.
  - Video information about ID, Thumbnail, Title, Description, Publish Date, Schedule Date, Actual Start Date, Actual End Date, Duration.

In order to maintain the integrity of the data we display, we only take data from the official API services. We do not scrape, and we do not collect anything that the platform does not expose publicly.

## Withdrawing access and deleting data

You can withdraw vTual's access to your Google and YouTube data at any time through the [Google security settings page](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), where you can review and revoke the access granted to connected applications.

To request deletion of the data vTual stores about your channel and content, contact us via our [Contact](/docs/silverspoon/contact) information and we will process the request.

## Third-party privacy policies

We integrate these third-party services into our website in order to inform you about your idol's activities and introduce content creators that you might like:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Twitch Privacy Policy](https://www.twitch.tv/p/en/legal/privacy-notice)

By using our services, you also agree and are bound by their privacy policy.

For everything that is not specific to these integrations, such as cookies, registration, and your rights over the information Silverspoon holds, read the platform [Privacy Policy](/docs/legal/privacy).
