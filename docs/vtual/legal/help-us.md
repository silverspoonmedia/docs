# Help Us

vTual is designed to bridge the gap between content creators and their audiences, making it easier for viewers to discover their favorite creators and vice versa. In essence, vTual aims to establish a platform and ecosystem that fosters positive interactions and growth for both creators and their fans.

However, vTual is not a charitable organization that gives everything to the public without expecting anything in return. Personally, the scale of vTual is too significant to be treated as a mere "build it and forget it later" project, because we want this project to get proper credit.

Therefore, in its operations, we hope this project receives the appropriate support it deserves. Such assistance is crucial for vTual to thrive and fulfill its mission effectively. This support will not only help sustain the project but also ensure it continues to evolve and adapt to the ever-changing landscape of content creation and consumption.

## Things you can do

There are numerous ways you can contribute to the growth of vTual. Here are a few suggestions.

### Feedback

Feedback is incredibly vital and essential for us. Share your thoughts on the implementation of our features, suggest additional functionality you might need, or simply express your appreciation for what we have accomplished.

Whatever it may be, your feedback can significantly clarify our work priorities and help us focus on what truly matters to our users. Your insights are invaluable in shaping the future of vTual, ensuring we continue to meet the needs of both content creators and their audiences effectively.

### Spread the word

Almost all of our expenditure is focused on infrastructure and development costs. We strive to secure the best server infrastructure that balances our needs within our budget, along with our personal expenses while working on this project. As a result, we currently allocate **0%** of our budget to promotional activities.

Therefore, if you believe this project is worth sustaining, we kindly ask for your support in spreading the word about vTual through word of mouth. Whether it is sharing information via social media posts, sending messages through chat services, or simply telling your story about us in a casual conversation with friends in the evening, every little bit helps.

### Donate

At this time, even though our needs and expenditures are quite tight, we do not require monetary assistance yet. We encourage you to save and use your funds for your personal needs instead.

Our main focus for now is building a strong foundation for vTual, and we appreciate your understanding and support. Contributions in the form of feedback, promotion, and engagement are far more valuable to us at this stage than financial support.
