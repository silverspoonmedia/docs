# Creator

vTual serves as a showcase of many content creators. To improve your browsing experience, we offer a personalized search feature that you can adjust to your needs.

![vTual creator page](/assets/docs/vtual/Creator.webp)

This makes it easy to find and explore content that aligns with your interests.

To find creators that match your preferences, combine various parameters in your search:

- Subscriber range
- Affiliation
- Type of content
- Language
- Gender

That flexibility helps you narrow down your options and discover creators who resonate with your specific tastes.
