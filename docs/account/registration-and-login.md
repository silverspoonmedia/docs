# Registration and Login

One account covers the whole platform. This page walks through creating it, confirming your email, and signing in.

## Registering

Open the register page and fill in three things:

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Full name | How you appear on the platform. |
| Email | Used for sign-in, verification, and account recovery. |
| Password | Entered twice to guard against typos. |

After submitting, we send a verification link to the email address you used. By registering, you agree to the platform [Terms of Service](/docs/legal/terms) and [Privacy Policy](/docs/legal/privacy).

:::note
Use an address you can keep long term. It is the only way back into your account if you forget your password.
:::

## Verifying your email

The verification link is valid for about an hour. Opening it confirms your address and unlocks the features that require a verified account, most importantly account recovery.

If the link has expired, or you never received it, request a new one from your account. The resend option sends a fresh link at any time, so a lost email is never a dead end.

## Signing in

Enter your email and password on the sign-in page. If your account has two-factor authentication enabled, a second screen asks for the current code from your authenticator app before you are let in.

There is also a **Remember me** option. Use it on a device you trust and you will not have to sign in again as often; leave it off on a shared computer.

| Situation | What happens |
|-----------|--------------|
| Wrong email or password | You are told the credentials do not match. The message does not reveal which part was wrong. |
| Two-factor enabled | You are asked for a code before the session starts. |
| Too many attempts | Sign-in is briefly throttled, and you are told how long to wait. |

## Signing out

Sign out from your account menu in the top bar. Your session ends immediately, and you are returned to a signed-out view.

Sessions are tied to your browser through a secure cookie. If you sign in from another tab or window, the same session is shared, and signing out in one place ends it everywhere in that browser.

:::tip
On a shared or public computer, sign out when you are finished. If you are unsure whether you left a session open elsewhere, changing your password is the reliable way to invalidate it.
:::
