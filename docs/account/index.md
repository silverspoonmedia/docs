# Account & Security

Your Silverspoon account works across every product on the platform. Sign in once to access Archivd, Microstock, and whatever arrives later. Security settings apply everywhere, saving you from managing several tiny kingdoms of credentials.

This section covers the parts of your account that are shared platform-wide. Product-specific settings live with that product's documentation.

## In this section

| Page | Covers |
|------|--------|
| [Registration and Login](/docs/account/registration-and-login) | Creating an account, verifying your email, signing in, and signing out. |
| [Two-Factor Authentication](/docs/account/two-factor-authentication) | Adding an authenticator app as a second step at sign-in. |
| [Connected Accounts](/docs/account/connected-accounts) | Linking Discord, and the YouTube and Twitch accounts it carries. |

## Where these settings live

Once you are signed in, your account menu in the top bar links to the pages above. On the platform these are:

| Setting | Path |
|---------|------|
| Change password | `/apps/profile/password` |
| Two-factor authentication | `/apps/profile/two-factor` |
| Connected accounts | `/apps/profile/connections` |

## Language

The interface is available in several languages. A language selector in the top bar switches your preference, and it is remembered on your account, so the choice follows you to other devices. Arabic is displayed right to left.

| Locale | Language |
|--------|----------|
| `en` | English |
| `id` | Indonesian |
| `ja` | Japanese |
| `ko` | Korean |

| `ar` | Arabic |
| `ru` | Russian |

## Security at a glance

- Your session is carried by a secure, HTTP-only cookie, so scripts on the page cannot read your sign-in token.
- Two-factor authentication is available to everyone and is the single biggest improvement you can make to your account security.
- Password reset links are short-lived, so an old email cannot be reused to take over an account.

:::tip
If you have not set up two-factor authentication yet, that is the first thing worth doing. See [Two-Factor Authentication](/docs/account/two-factor-authentication).
:::
