# Introduction

Welcome to the Silverspoon helpdesk portal. This is the home base for everything we build, document, and support.

Before you dive into the details, here is a quick introduction to who we are. It should take no more than five minutes.

## What Silverspoon is

Silverspoon is a collection of products created and maintained by **Kurokuma Wakaba** (an initial, not a legal name). For the most part, each application is built single-handedly.

All of them run as one platform rather than as separate sites. There is a single codebase and a single entry point at `silverspoon.me`, and each product lives under its own path, such as `silverspoon.me/vtual`. The capabilities that every product shares, such as authentication and billing, live in one core layer that products build on.

Most products are published on the internet and can be used publicly. The codebase is mostly closed source, though several open source projects may appear in the future.

Our products are primarily everyday applications built on a **SaaS (Software as a Service)** model. We rarely build something tied to a single person or company, simply because we enjoy the SaaS model.

- **Short-term goal:** serve these projects as a portfolio of our skills.
- **Long-term goal:** grow them into mature, product-driven businesses.

:::tip
We are always open to new possibilities and collaborations.
:::

## Terminology

The name "Silverspoon" is not just a label; it carries meaning. As described on [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_spoon), the term "silver spoon" describes someone born into a prosperous background, often implying that their advantages were inherited rather than earned.

That citation is abstract, so here is the actual reason we chose the name: we want everyone who has a relationship with Silverspoon, whether owner, user, or partner, to receive goodness and prosperity. Just like the silver spoon itself, we all can be the silver spoon.

That is why SaaS is the business model that fits us best for now.

## Products

Only active products are listed here. Professionally, we create and run the following:

### vTual

vTual simplifies the process of keeping up with your favorite content creators, all in one convenient portal. Content creators publish across different services, and manually checking where each piece of content lives is a hassle.

The service is open to anyone. If you are a content creator, you can register your account directly and add it to our tracking database without manual approval. Add links, change your avatar, manage your biodata, or modify your channel; the choice is yours.

If you need help with anything, we are happy to assist. Start with the [vTual documentation](/docs/vtual/about).

### GetLanded

GetLanded is an open-source product that simplifies building landing pages for individuals, groups, and companies. Instead of plain HTML, it is built on the **Laravel** framework, so you can add custom system logic whenever you need it.

That flexibility also means you can lean on the large Laravel ecosystem for references and documentation, instead of relying only on what Silverspoon provides.

- **Free for everyone**, whether individuals or businesses.
- **Source code is public and unencrypted**, which encourages collaboration, transparency, and continuous improvement.
- **Deployable on serverless platforms**, an affordable option if you would rather not maintain your own server.
- **Runs on LAMP, LEMP, or similar stacks** if you prefer your own infrastructure.

:::note
Docker support is not available yet, but the existing deployment options already cover most needs.
:::

:::warning
GetLanded is still developed internally and is not yet available to the public. Stay tuned for further updates.
:::
