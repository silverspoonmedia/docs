# Frequently Asked Questions

This section answers the questions that are likely to be asked most often about Silverspoon as a platform. Consider this a head start before you have to ask.

:::info[TL;DR]
A collection of the platform-level questions we expect to come up frequently, answered up front.
:::

## Who is behind this project?

Silverspoon is created, developed, and managed by a programmer and content creator named **Kurokuma Wakaba**, or simply **Waka**.

Currently, it operates as a one-man show for various reasons, mainly because cash flow is still allocated to development and server infrastructure, leaving no monetary allocation to pay other members. That allows Waka to maintain full control over the project's direction and execution.

There is always the possibility of bringing in additional team members in the future, should the budget allow.

## Is this platform free to use?

Currently, all the features offered by our projects are available for free, allowing users to take full advantage of what the projects provide without any cost. However, in the future, there may be certain features accessible only through a one-time purchase or a subscription fee.

Even though "offering all features for free" looks generous, considering that development, maintenance, and operations incur enormous costs, it becomes essential to explore healthy and sustainable business models.

We do not wish to adopt a "burning money" strategy either, which has proven unsustainable. Hence these projects are not 100% free.

Nevertheless, the core features will remain free and will not be commercialized, as our main commitment is to ensure all users have access to essential functionality without any cost.

We will only commercialize additional or cosmetic features, which allows us to enhance the user experience without compromising the core functionality of our services.

For how paid features are billed and taxed, see [Payments and Taxation](/docs/billing/payments-and-taxation).

## Will there be multilingual support?

That is the initial plan. To optimize development time, the focus will be on adding languages predominantly used by users from countries where our products are most frequently used.

By prioritizing these languages, we aim to enhance accessibility and user experience for the core audience.

Given the limited number of team members working on these projects, translation tools and AI assistance may be used to ensure grammatical accuracy during the translation process.

If you are interested in contributing, your involvement could be incredibly beneficial. Collaborating with others who share a passion for these projects can enhance the quality of our translations and ensure we capture the nuances of each language effectively.

## Are the services down?

In this imperfect world, despite all efforts to maintain uptime, downtime inevitably occurs from time to time.

Before asking or waiting for an official announcement about service disruption, you can refer to the following resources regarding the infrastructure status used by our services.

### Status page

If you experience any service interruptions, visit [our status page](https://status.silverspoon.me). There you will find comprehensive information regarding the overall history and current status of the servers used by all Silverspoon projects. All servers are monitored and checked every minute.

This page keeps you informed and provides transparency about any issues that may arise, ensuring you are always in the loop regarding the performance of our services.

### Ping result

Another tool you can use to check the status of a product's services is a ping result, such as [our ping result](https://ping.pe/www.vtual.net) for vTual. This platform provides real-time ping results for the product from various Internet Service Providers (ISPs) across different countries and continents.

By using this tool, you gain valuable insight into the accessibility and performance of our services from various locations, helping you understand how well the service is functioning in your area and beyond. This additional resource can be particularly useful for troubleshooting and assessing connectivity issues between your localhost and the service.

### Moving forward

If all resources indicate that the service is running smoothly and is accessible, try accessing it using incognito mode or as a guest in your browser. This approach can eliminate potential issues related to cached data or cookies that might be affecting your browsing experience.

If you have tried all the suggested methods and verified that everything is functioning correctly, yet you still cannot access the service, reach out to us for further assistance. There may be an underlying issue that requires our attention, and we are here to help you resolve it.

:::note[Example]
While the server used by a product may be online, incorrect configurations could potentially block users from accessing the service.
:::

Your experience is important to us, and we want to ensure you can access our services without hindrance.

## Are the services going to end?

Every endeavor that has a beginning inevitably has an end, and that holds true for our projects as well. However, there is no need to worry, as that conclusion is not imminent. There is still so much we wish to accomplish and demonstrate through these projects.

That said, it is also essential to balance dreams with a reality check. If we reach a point where, despite all the effort and dedication poured into a project, it fails to yield positive results personally (an assessment not limited to monetary reasons), that could serve as a decisive moment for determining the project's future.

## Is that all?

Feel free to ask any questions, and we will respond here so that others can benefit from the information as well. This is a great way to share knowledge and ensure everyone has access to the answers they may be seeking.

Looking for something specific to a product? Each product keeps its own FAQ, such as the [vTual FAQ](/docs/vtual/legal/faq).
