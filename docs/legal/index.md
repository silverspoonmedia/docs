# Legal

Silverspoon is one internal office platform. This page explains where legal information lives and which document applies to what.

:::info[TL;DR]
The terms and the privacy policy on this page cover the whole platform. There is no separate product-level layer, because the platform is the only thing that ships.
:::

## How legal documents are organized

Legal information sits in one layer, the platform itself.

- **Platform level** applies to everyone who signs in. These are the [Terms of Service](/docs/legal/terms) and the [Privacy Policy](/docs/legal/privacy).
- **FAQ** answers the platform-level questions we expect to be asked most often, such as who is behind Silverspoon, who may use it, and what to do when a service is down.

If a new capability ever needs wording of its own, it is added here rather than as a separate site.

## Platform level

### Terms of Service

The [Terms of Service](/docs/legal/terms) govern your use of the platform, including accounts, prohibited uses, intellectual property, copyright claims, and liability.

### Privacy Policy

The [Privacy Policy](/docs/legal/privacy) explains what we collect, how we use it, and how you control it, including what information we require for account registration.

### Frequently Asked Questions

The [FAQ](/docs/legal/faq) answers the platform-level questions we expect to be asked most often, such as who is behind Silverspoon, whether it is for sale, and what to do when a service is down.

## Jurisdiction

All Silverspoon projects comply with the regulations of the **Republic of Indonesia**. As a user, you are also expected to follow these regulations. See [Introduction](/docs/intro#jurisdiction) for the reasoning.

## Governing law

Terms are governed and construed in accordance with the laws of Indonesia, without regard to conflict of law provisions.

## Legal contact

Legal notices, copyright infringement claims, and related correspondence go to `contact@spnd.uk`. See [Contact](/docs/silverspoon/contact) for the full list of ways to reach us.
