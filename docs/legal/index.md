# Legal

Silverspoon is a single platform that hosts multiple products. This page explains where legal information lives and which document applies to what.

:::info[TL;DR]
Platform-level terms and privacy policy live here and apply to every product. A product may add its own document for things that are genuinely product-specific, such as third-party integrations, and those supplement the platform documents.
:::

## How legal documents are layered

Legal information is organized in two layers.

1. **Platform level** applies to everyone who uses any Silverspoon product. These are the [Terms of Service](/docs/legal/terms) and the [Privacy Policy](/docs/legal/privacy).
2. **Product level** covers matters that only exist for one product, such as how it integrates with a third-party platform. A product document supplements the platform documents and, where it conflicts with them on that product's subject matter, takes precedence for that product.

When in doubt, read the product document first for the specific subject, then fall back to the platform documents for everything else.

## Platform level

### Terms of Service

The [Terms of Service](/docs/legal/terms) govern your use of the platform and every product it hosts, including accounts, prohibited uses, intellectual property, copyright claims, and liability.

### Privacy Policy

The [Privacy Policy](/docs/legal/privacy) explains what we collect, how we use it, and how you control it, including what information we require for account registration.

### Frequently Asked Questions

The [FAQ](/docs/legal/faq) answers the platform-level questions we expect to be asked most often, such as who is behind Silverspoon, whether it is free, and what to do when a service is down.

## Product level

Each product publishes its own documents for matters that are specific to it, because data handling, tracked services, and third-party integrations differ per product. For a given product, the platform documents still apply; these documents cover the product-specific parts.

### vTual

- [Product Terms](/docs/vtual/legal/tos) — the terms specific to vTual, on top of the platform Terms of Service.
- [Privacy Notice: Third-Party Platforms](/docs/vtual/legal/privacy) — how vTual uses the Google, YouTube, and Twitch APIs, and how to withdraw access.
- [Product FAQ](/docs/vtual/legal/faq) — questions specific to the vTual project.

## Jurisdiction

All Silverspoon projects comply with the regulations of the **Republic of Indonesia**. As a user, you are also expected to follow these regulations. See [Introduction](/docs/intro#jurisdiction) for the reasoning.

## Governing law

Terms are governed and construed in accordance with the laws of Indonesia, without regard to conflict of law provisions.

## Legal contact

Legal notices, copyright infringement claims, and related correspondence go to `contact@spnd.uk`. See [Contact](/docs/silverspoon/contact) for the full list of ways to reach us.

## Adding a product

When a new product is documented, it publishes its own product-level legal documents in its product folder and is linked from this page. A product-specific document takes precedence over the platform-level documents for that product's subject matter.
