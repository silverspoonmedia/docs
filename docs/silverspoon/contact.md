# Contact

Welcome to Silverspoon. There are several ways to reach Silverspoon, so pick whichever is most convenient. Social media, email, or online chat all work; carrier pigeons remain unofficial.

## General inquiries

For questions or concerns about Silverspoon products and services, reach out any time. You can send a direct message or mention Silverspoon on [Twitter](https://www.vtual.net/go/twitter).

When the situation allows, I plan to open a Discord server as well. It will be an ideal place for support, feedback, or simply connecting with others who share your interests. I hope to see you there.

## Business inquiries

Business communication is best handled privately by email at `contact@spnd.uk`.

:::warning
For security reasons, all incoming inquiries are sorted manually, so replies may take a while.
:::

Email is not only more formal, it also leaves a written record of potential discussions between Silverspoon and your community or company. That record is valuable for referencing key points and agreements later.

:::danger[Important]
If you have communicated with Silverspoon before, reply to that existing thread instead of sending a new email to `contact@spnd.uk`. Otherwise your message goes back into the sorting queue.
:::

If your inquiry is urgent, mention it on Twitter so I can prioritize it.

I encourage you to reach out with inquiries or proposals. I am always open to exploring opportunities for collaboration and growth.
